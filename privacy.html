Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

This policy describes how Bloop & Friends ("we", "the channel") handles information on this website and in Bloop Publisher, a private tool the channel owner uses to publish videos to the channel's own YouTube account.

1. Use of YouTube API Services

Bloop Publisher uses YouTube API Services (the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API) to upload the channel's own videos, schedule their publication, set thumbnails, manage playlists, and read the channel's own aggregate statistics. By using YouTube content you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

2. Who uses Bloop Publisher

Bloop Publisher is an internal tool. It is used only by the channel owner, on the owner's own computer, to manage the owner's own YouTube channel. It is not offered, sold or distributed to anyone else and has no public sign-up.

3. Information we access

  • The owner's Google account authorization (OAuth token), granted by the owner on Google's consent screen, so the tool can act on the owner's channel.
  • The channel's own video and playlist information (video IDs, titles, publish times) returned by the API after an upload.
  • The channel's own aggregate analytics (for example total views and watch time per video).

We do not access, collect or store data about other YouTube users, other channels, or viewers, including children who watch our videos. Our videos are designated "made for kids", and we do not collect personal information from children.

4. How we use and share information

The information above is used only to publish and organize the channel's own videos and to decide which lessons to make next. We do not sell, rent or share this information with any third party and do not use it for advertising.

5. Storage and security

The OAuth token and the list of uploaded video IDs are stored only on the channel owner's computer. Analytics figures are kept for no more than 30 days and are then refreshed or deleted, in line with the YouTube API Services Developer Policies.

6. Deleting data and revoking access

Access granted to Bloop Publisher can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. When access is revoked, or on request, all stored tokens and data obtained from YouTube API Services are deleted within 7 days.

7. This website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising and does not collect any personal information. Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts. Videos are watched on YouTube under YouTube's own terms and privacy policy.

8. Changes

We will update this page if our practices change. The date at the top shows the latest version.
